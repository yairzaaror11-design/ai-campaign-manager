The revised master authorizes autonomous development through S0–S10; old S0-only stopping instructions are superseded. Production and new financial/external effects remain gates. Live CRM/billing connection remains excluded; offline contracts can be developed independently.

|S9|Connector gaps documented.|One supported platform at a time after eligibility.|

Every slice needs code, tests, reviewable PR, evidence and explicit limitations. Continue independent safe development while access gates are pending; never replace missing capabilities with marketing claims or invented forecasts.
